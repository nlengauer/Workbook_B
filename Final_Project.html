Final Project ★

This is the stage of the final project we are currently working on. The beanie will include all of the components we explored during class, such as: motion sensors, LEDs connected by conductive wiring, speakers and more. There will be soundscapes that we will have created which will play when the PIR sensors detect movement. The biggest consideration at the moment in the safety and construction of the beanie so that the wires stay on the breadboard and noone's hair sets of fire. But at this stage we are progressing well.
